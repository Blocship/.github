**What you're actually paying for.** We optimise for your outcome, not our billable hours. A client once asked for a mobile app, roughly sixty hours of work from scratch. We got the same result running on the stack they already had in about two.

**When we're not the right fit.** If you need a fifty-person body shop, pure staff augmentation, or someone who'll just nod along, that isn't us. We're a small senior team, and we'd rather say so on the first call.

1. You send the brief, as rough or as detailed as you've got.
2. Shahzad replies within 24 hours with first thoughts.
3. We jump on a call and screen-share the real thing.
4. You get a clear scope, timeline and price, or a straight no.
